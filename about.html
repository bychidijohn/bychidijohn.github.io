About

A bit about who I am and what I work on.

I'm a senior ML engineer / applied scientist focused on personalization, causal inference, and incentive optimization. I've spent the last several years building systems that decide who gets which promotion, notification, or experience — and measuring whether those decisions actually moved the business.

What I work on

Uplift modeling, off-policy evaluation for contextual bandits, experimentation design for two-sided marketplaces, and the unglamorous middle ground between offline metrics and online business outcomes.

What I write about

The stuff that's hard to find in papers and harder to find in blog posts — practitioner-level depth on causal ML for growth, with code that runs and conclusions that are honest about what didn't work.
